Short pieces reveal whether a layout is confident or merely loud.

Margin note to self

Opening

Post 05 is a reminder that even a short article still benefits from a strong frame. When the content is modest, the layout has even less to hide behind.

That makes placeholder writing useful in a very direct way: it reveals whether the page feels worth reading before the argument has earned that feeling.

Notes

This mock page keeps the structure stable while varying only the read time and numbering. Small differences like that are enough to make placeholders more useful without making maintenance awkward.

For a design pass, that is usually the right tradeoff.
